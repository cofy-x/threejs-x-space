# Octopus character asset

`src/assets/octo.glb` is an original procedural octopus sculpt created for this experience in Blender through Blender MCP. Its geometry, vertex pigments, and normal texture are generated by `scripts/build_octo.py` and distributed under this repository's MIT license. No downloaded mesh or third-party image is packaged in the asset.

The [octopus closeup published by The Rookies](https://d3stdg5so273ei.cloudfront.net/ghost/content/images/2023/08/nuke_octo_render-1.jpg) was studied for continuous mantle anatomy, fleshy eyelids, layered skin detail, and a focused expression. It is a visual reference only; its pixels and geometry are not used in this model. The original character here uses coral skin, restrained chromatophore mottling, a violet skirt, amber irises, and horizontal pupils.

## Rebuild

For direct mesh or material adjustments, open the [saved Blender source](docs/blender/octo.blend) and follow the [authoring and export guide](docs/blender/README.md). The compressed source contains the editable mantle and eyes with a packed normal texture. The eight tentacles, cube, and underwater studio remain owned by the Three.js implementation.

Run Blender from the repository root:

```sh
blender --background --python packages/experience-cube/scripts/build_octo.py
```

The script creates its own `Octo Character Studio` scene and replaces only that generated scene when rerun. All other open scenes remain intact and are excluded from the export. It writes the GLB directly into the owning package. Set `OCTO_OUTPUT` to override the destination or `OCTO_STAGE=blockout` to omit skin microdetail for silhouette review. The same script can run in an open Blender instance through Blender MCP.

## Sculpt and materials

The mantle, orbital saddle, fleshy eyelids, and scalloped skirt are fused with voxel remeshing, smoothed, and reduced to a real-time mesh. Small surface papillae are part of the geometry. Pigmentation is stored as vertex colors; a generated, embedded 512 × 512 normal map supplies finer skin pores. Procedural Blender shader nodes are not required at runtime.

Five shared PBR materials use controlled roughness and clearcoat. Radially striated iris geometry and a separate dark pupil provide readable eyes at close range. The diffuse palette matches the runtime tentacles. All eight arms and their suction cups are constructed and animated by the Three.js scene.

## Runtime contract

- Coordinates: Y up, facing positive Z.
- Root: `Octo`, with identity transform.
- Mantle pivot: `OctoMantle`, at `[0, 1.25, 0]`.
- Unified skin surface: `OctoSkin`.
- Eye pivots: `EyeLeft` and `EyeRight`, at `[±0.445, 0.22, 0.28]` relative to the mantle pivot, or Y = 1.47 in world space.
- Skirt attaches to the runtime arms around Y = 0.65; the crown reaches approximately Y = 2.30.
- Approximately 2.05 MB, 60,998 triangles, five materials, and nine material primitives.
- One embedded PNG normal map; standard uncompressed glTF 2.0 geometry. No external texture requests or geometry decoder.

The GLB was inspected in Blender from front and side views during separate silhouette, eye placement, and material passes. Surface contrast was reduced after inspection in the integrated browser scene so the skin retains smooth light gradients at normal viewing distance.
